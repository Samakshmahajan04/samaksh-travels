Cab Booking

Udhampur to Srinagar cab, comfortable for the long road.

The Udhampur to Srinagar drive is a full day through the mountains. We arrange the right vehicle and a driver who knows the route, for families, couples and groups, one-way or as part of a longer Kashmir trip.

What we can arrange

  • Udhampur to Srinagar one-way or round trip.
  • Sedan, SUV or tempo traveller for families and groups.
  • Local sightseeing in Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg with the same driver.
  • Hotels and houseboat stays booked alongside the cab.
  • Pickup from Jammu airport or railway station if you are arriving from outside.

Good to know

The road from Udhampur to Srinagar runs through the Pir Panjal mountains and is a long day on the road, so we plan the departure time and breaks with you. Highway closures and weather can change timings, particularly in winter, and we check with you before travel. Please carry a valid photo ID for every traveller.

How it works

  1. Message us your dates, number of people and where you want to go in Kashmir.

  4. We send your itinerary and driver details before you leave.

Common questions

Can you arrange a cab from Udhampur to Srinagar?

Yes. We arrange one-way and round-trip cabs and tempo travellers, and can include sightseeing in Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg.

Can you also book hotels in Srinagar?

Yes. Tell us your dates, guests and budget and we will suggest and confirm a stay.

Is the road open in winter?

Road conditions change with snow and weather. We check the situation before your travel date and tell you honestly if timings may change.

Can I plan this from another city?

Yes. Most bookings happen over phone and WhatsApp for travellers anywhere in India.
