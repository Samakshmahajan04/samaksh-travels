Kashmir Tours

Kashmir tours from Udhampur, planned around you.

Dal Lake, the meadows of Gulmarg, the valleys of Pahalgam and Sonamarg: we plan Kashmir trips to match your dates, group and budget, with the cab, stays and sightseeing handled in one conversation.

Places we plan

  • Srinagar: Dal Lake, shikara rides, gardens and old city.
  • Gulmarg: meadows in summer and snow in winter.
  • Pahalgam: river valleys and easy walks.
  • Sonamarg: mountain scenery and glacier views.

What we arrange

  • Cab or tempo traveller for the whole trip, with one driver.
  • Hotels and houseboat stays to suit your budget.
  • Flights and trains to Jammu or Srinagar.
  • Custom itineraries for families, couples, honeymooners and groups.

Best time to go

Spring and summer are good for green valleys and open meadows, autumn for colour, and winter for snow, especially in Gulmarg. Tell us what you want to see and we will suggest the right months. We do not publish fixed package prices yet: every trip is quoted for your dates and group, with inclusions listed before you pay anything.

How it works

  1. Message us your dates, number of people and the places you like.
  2. We suggest a day-by-day plan and send a clear quote.
  3. You confirm, and we book the cab, stays and tickets.
  4. We send your itinerary and stay with you on WhatsApp during the trip.

Common questions

How many days do I need for Kashmir?

It depends on what you want to see. Tell us your dates and interests and we will build a plan that fits, whether it is a short break or a longer trip.

Do you offer fixed packages with prices?

Not yet. Each trip is planned and quoted for your dates and group, and the quote lists what is and is not included.

Can you arrange everything from Udhampur?

Yes: the cab, hotels and houseboat stay, flights and trains. You deal with one team.

Is Kashmir suitable for families and seniors?

Yes, with the right plan. Tell us about the ages in your group and we will keep the pace and stays comfortable.
