# WhatsApp auto-replies (WhatsApp Business app, free)

Every message from the website ends with "(Sent from samakshtravels.com)".

Setup: install **WhatsApp Business** on the phone with 94199 61983 -> Settings -> Business tools.

## 1. Greeting message (sent automatically to new customers) — Recipients: Everyone
Hello! Thanks for contacting *Samaksh Travels*, Udhampur. 🙏
To make sure this is a genuine enquiry, please reply *YES* with your name and travel date. We'll look at your request right away.

## 2. Away message (outside working hours)
Thanks for messaging Samaksh Travels! We're currently away. We'll reply as soon as we're back. For urgent help call 94199 61983.

## 3. Quick replies (type the shortcut, tap to send)
/checking : Thank you for confirming! 🙏 Please give us a moment — we're checking your details and will get back to you shortly with the best options.
/details : To prepare your quote please share: travelling from/to, date(s), number of travellers, and budget (if any).
/thanks : Thank you for choosing Samaksh Travels! Safe travels ✈️🚆

## Flow
1. Customer messages from the site -> greeting asks them to reply YES.
2. When they reply YES, type */checking* and send.
3. Then reply with the quote.

A fully automatic YES->reply flow needs the paid WhatsApp Business API (a chatbot provider). Not set up.
