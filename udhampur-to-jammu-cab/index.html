What we can arrange

  • Airport transfers between Udhampur and Jammu airport, timed to your flight.
  • Railway station pickups and drops at Jammu Tawi and Udhampur.
  • One-way or round trip, with waiting time if you need it.
  • Sedan, SUV or tempo traveller depending on your group size and luggage.
  • Onward travel from Jammu to Katra, Patnitop or Srinagar in the same booking.

Good to know

The road distance between Udhampur and Jammu is roughly 65 km, and the drive usually takes about an hour and a half to two hours, depending on traffic, weather and road work on the highway. For a flight or train, we suggest leaving with a comfortable buffer, and we will help you work out the timing.

How it works

  1. Message us the date, pickup point, drop point and number of people.
  2. We reply with a clear quote that lists what is and is not included.
  3. You confirm, and we book the cab.
  4. We send the driver and vehicle details before your pickup.

Common questions

Yes. Share your flight time and we will plan the pickup so you reach the airport with a comfortable buffer.

We arrange sedans, SUVs and tempo travellers. Tell us your group size and luggage and we will suggest the right one.

Can I book from outside Udhampur?

Yes. Most bookings happen over phone and WhatsApp.
