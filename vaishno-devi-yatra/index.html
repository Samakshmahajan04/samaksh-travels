Vaishno Devi Yatra

Vaishno Devi yatra from Udhampur, planned end to end.

Udhampur is one of the closest towns to Katra, the base of the Vaishno Devi yatra. We are a local travel agency here, so we can arrange your ride, stay and tickets in one conversation, with a real person answering on WhatsApp.

What we can arrange

  • Cab or tempo traveller from Udhampur, Jammu railway station or Jammu airport to Katra, and back.
  • Hotel stay in Katra to suit your group size and budget.
  • Train and flight tickets to Jammu or Udhampur, plus onward travel.
  • Helicopter booking help and guidance on how the process works.
  • Combined trips that add Patnitop, Jammu temples or Kashmir after the yatra.

How it works

  1. Message us your dates, number of people and starting point.
  2. We reply with a clear quote that lists what is and is not included.
  3. You confirm, and we book the cab, hotel and tickets.
  4. We send your itinerary and the driver's details before you travel.

Before you go

Yatra registration is required by the Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Shrine Board, so complete it through the official channels before you set out. Carry a valid photo ID for every traveller. Weather and operator schedules can change plans, so we confirm timings with you close to your travel date.

Common questions

Can you arrange a cab from Udhampur to Katra?

Yes. Cabs and tempo travellers run from Udhampur to Katra, and to and from Jammu railway station and airport. Share the date, group size and timing for a quote.

Do you book hotels in Katra?

Yes. Tell us your dates, guests and budget and we will suggest and confirm a stay.

Can you help with helicopter booking?

We help you plan around it. Availability and rules are set by the operator, so we tell you the current process when you ask.

Can I plan from outside Udhampur?

Yes. Most bookings happen over phone and WhatsApp for travellers anywhere in India.
